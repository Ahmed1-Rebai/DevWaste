# Sound effect credits

Both files are copied unchanged from the bundled HyperFrames `media-use` SFX
library, which sources them from [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/)
under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/)
(commercial and non-commercial use, no attribution required).

- `alert-error.mp3` (library key `error`): plays when the first ALERT row appears.
- `whoosh-short.mp3` (library key `whoosh-short`): the two upward whip transitions.
